# Post 3 — Mastodon (launch day)

> Stop writing 20-line bash loops around your coding agent.
>
> A naive loop can't tell "agent said DONE" from "work is actually correct" —
> and an agent that can see your test cases will overfit to them.
>
> AgentLoop (MIT, free) ships a held-out oracle: reference captured on 200
> inputs, 197 sealed away from the agent, grade demands a perfect score on the
> ones it never saw. SWE-bench-Verified: 64% → 80% for Claude Code.
>
